Don’t Forget the Fees

SELECTED
Orin
Oh, fees. Don’t get me started about those, dear. I have seen so many promising strategies quietly being dismantled by fees. So many. It hurts a little just thinking about them.

See, the thing is: gross opportunity is not net profit. That distinction matters enormously, and yet it is remarkable how often it gets overlooked until it is too late. Have you incorporated all applicable transaction fees into your calculations? Because a strategy that looks attractive before costs can look very different once they are included. Sometimes dramatically so.

Pay particular attention to high-turnover approaches. They tend to accumulate fees in a way that is easy to underestimate when you are focused on the individual trade rather than the aggregate cost.

Ask yourself whether fee structures change when volumes change. If they do, that variation should influence where you allocate capital, not as an afterthought, but as a core part of the calculation. Performance should always be measured after costs, dear. Not just before. Before is just a number. After is the truth.
